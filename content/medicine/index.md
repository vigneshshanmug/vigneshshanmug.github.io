Vignesh is a staff pathologist in the Department of Pathology at Brigham and Women's Hospital and an Instructor in Pathology at Harvard Medical School. He practices **hematopathology** and **molecular pathology**: the diagnosis of lymphomas, leukemias and related disorders of the blood, bone marrow and lymphoid tissues, integrating morphology, immunophenotyping and genomic testing.

He trained in anatomic pathology at Brigham and Women's Hospital and completed the Geraldine S. Pinkus Fellowship in Hematopathology there. His clinical and translational research centers on molecular diagnostics, histiocytic and dendritic cell neoplasms, and diagnostic lymphoma pathology.
